# v0.2 — current-action category precedence

## Observed v0.1 weakness

The three uploaded independent v0.1 responses are retained byte-for-byte under
`responses/`. All three pass the original validator and the full frozen output
schema. Structural validity does not resolve the observed analytical inconsistency.

Primary begins its recommendation with `DEFER` because permission, service terms
and other prerequisites are unresolved, describing an experiment as a future step.
Contrast 1 begins `BOUNDED EXPERIMENT, conditional on pre-activation gates`, while
also requiring service/data/IP terms, scope authorization and containment to be
verified before activation, and says to defer activation if gates remain unverified.
Contrast 2 selects `DEFER` for permission, containment and resource gaps.

The startup has materially stronger assumed capabilities and a cleared workspace.
Those facts support a potentially easier path to a trial, but do not themselves
resolve the expressly unverified service configuration/terms. The inconsistency
is between a current category and a conditional future action, rather than a
demonstrated failure to preserve general technology findings. The original rule
already addressed essential unconfirmed controls in the learning section; the
revision makes its precedence explicit at category selection. It does not establish
that every difference between organizations is erroneous or that every planning
detail must block learning.

the present primary category from a conditional future PoC/experiment and requires
