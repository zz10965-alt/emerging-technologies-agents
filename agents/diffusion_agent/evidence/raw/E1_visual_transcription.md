# Verified visual transcription — selected adoption panels only

Original: E1_infographic.pdf, single page. Rendered locally with PyMuPDF and visually inspected. pdftotext produced no readable text because the infographic uses outlined text. This file is an analyst transcription, not another survey or an independent source. Original image/PDF takes precedence.

Panel 01: “90% of technology professionals report using AI at work.”
Panel 04: “60% of AI users surveyed use AI ‘about half the time or more’ when encountering a problem to solve or task to complete, suggesting that AI has become a frequent part of the development process.”
Footer: “This year’s research includes more than 100 hours of qualitative data and survey responses from nearly 5,000 technology professionals from around the world.”

Only these numerical adoption statements are admitted from this infographic. The headline “virtually ubiquitous,” productivity perceptions, platform-adoption percentages and causal/promotional wording are not adopted as population facts. The 2025 instrument identifies the frequency question’s reference period as “In the last 3 months.” The 60% base is AI users surveyed; it is not the same denominator as all technology professionals, hospitals or firms. The infographic does not disclose per-item n, sampling procedure, weights, exact collection dates, attrition or longitudinal retention. No 2025 recruitment methodology is inferred from the inspected 2024 report.
